CASE STUDY // TIME MANAGEMENT — FOCUSFLOW

01 · The Problem

Poor time management affects students and people in general. The main causes and the consequences it leads to:

CAUSES
  • Procrastination
  • Difficulty prioritizing tasks
  • Difficulty estimating the time needed for tasks
  • Fear of not completing tasks
  • Social media and phone distractions
  • Difficulty dealing with difficult or multiple tasks
CONSEQUENCES
  • Missed tasks
  • Stress
  • Lower productivity
  • Wasted time
  • Anxiety
  • Lower academic performance

02 · Research & Data Collection

We researched the problem to understand the causes and effects of poor time management and to identify the most common problems people face.

99Online survey responses
15–35Age range of respondents
2Methods: online survey + offline interviews with classmates
